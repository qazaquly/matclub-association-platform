# Roadmap after the completed Phase 2 management platform

Phase 1 established the institutional website, membership lifecycle, profiles, branch operations, central administration, and immutable history. Phase 2.1 added dynamic CMS content; Phase 2.2 added the Event core, public calendar, structured results, and Event-News links; Phase 2.3 added member registration, invited guests, attendance, reception, seating, and profile activity history. The next completed block adds full internal Project management and writes confirmed Project participation into that same profile history.

The unified Branch workspace, multi-criteria people search, operational reports, management indicators, and permanent internal institutional document registry are also complete. The agreed Phase 2 scope is therefore closed. Phase 3 should begin only after real operational data is sufficient to justify deeper analytics and new workflows.

## Phase 2 candidates

- membership fees, invoices, and payment status;
- full branch workspace combining members, Events, Projects, results, documents, and periodic reports — completed;
- internal institutional document registry — completed;
- filtered operational reports with Excel/PDF export — completed;
- management indicators with branch comparison and period trends — completed;
- professional tags and expertise classification;
- publications and a searchable methodological library;
- notifications and communication preferences;
- expanded configurable application fields and document requirements;
- operational reporting for branch coordinators.

## Phase 3 candidates

- expert and judge registries for professional assignments (not competition management);
- mentorship matching and program history;
- cross-project contribution history;
- deeper branch performance scoring after sufficient operational data accumulates;
- advanced analytics and longitudinal institutional reporting;
- data-retention automation, archival exports, and governance workflows;
- external-system integrations through versioned APIs.

## Explicitly out of scope

No phase of this repository should silently become an olympiad platform. Competition testing, scoring, protocols, certificates, diplomas, and competition management remain external-system responsibilities unless a future governance decision creates a separate integration project.
